Questions

Do guests get sent to a Google Form?

No. The RSVP form is part of your invitation website. Replies go to your Google Sheet behind the scenes.

Can I see replies live?

Yes. Each answer shows up in your Google Sheet within seconds. Download it as Excel or CSV any time.

Can I change details after sharing?

Yes. Send me the changes and I update the live site.

Who hosts the website?

I deploy it on Vercel for you, and can connect your own domain.
